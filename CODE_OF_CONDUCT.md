# Code of conduct

We want project discussions to be useful, precise, and welcoming.

- Discuss evidence and behavior rather than making assumptions about people.
- Give actionable feedback and explain the technical tradeoff.
- Do not harass, insult, threaten, or publish another person's private information.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about project scope and duplicated work.

Unacceptable behavior may be reported through GitHub's private reporting channels. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary.
